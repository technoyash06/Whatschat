WhatsChat V15 - Reliable Voice and Video Calls

WHAT IS NEW
- Fixed the black call screen shown after another person joined.
- Call connections start immediately instead of waiting for microphone/camera permission.
- If direct WebRTC is blocked, live audio/video automatically switches through the WhatsChat WSS gateway.
- Click FIX CONNECTION in the call platform to switch to the gateway relay immediately.
- Click START SOUND when the browser blocks incoming audio autoplay.
- Create a persistent group with several registered usernames.
- Open MY GROUPS to see every group name, code, owner, and member count.
- Group owners can add members or delete the group.
- Other members can leave a group.
- Group messages are delivered live and saved for members who are signed out.
- Members added later cannot see messages sent before they joined.
- Group chats support typed or pasted text, emojis, GIF links, and animated stickers.
- MEDIA now works inside groups for audio messages, camera photos, videos, files, HTML downloads, contact cards, polls, and GIFs.
- VOICE and VIDEO now invite every online group member. Each person answers or declines independently.
- People who answer stay in the call; people who decline do not join. If everyone declines, the call ends.
- Group calls include microphone, camera, and screen-sharing controls.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP into its own new folder.
2. Double-click 1_OPEN_WHATSCHAT_V15.cmd.
3. Wait for the fresh public page to open. Cloudflare can take up to two minutes.
4. On that page, click Open the Public WhatsChat Project.
5. Click the green flag, then LOGIN or REGISTER.

HOW TO CREATE A GROUP
1. Every person needs to register a username first.
2. On the home screen, click CREATE GROUP.
3. Type a group name.
4. Type the usernames separated by commas, for example: Alex,Maya,Sam
5. Open MY GROUPS. Your new group and its 8-character group code appear there.

HOW TO USE GROUPS
- OPEN GROUP: enter the group code shown in MY GROUPS.
- ADD MEMBER: group owners can add another registered username.
- LEAVE / DELETE: members can leave; only the owner can delete the group.
- Inside a group, use SEND, EMOJI, or GIF just like a direct chat.
- Inside either a direct chat or group chat, use MEDIA, VOICE, and VIDEO at the top.

OTHER FEATURES INCLUDED
- Saved contacts and automatic saving when someone messages you.
- Offline direct messages and group messages.
- Delivered/read symbols.
- Voice messages, camera, photos, videos, files, safe HTML downloads, contacts, polls, and GIF generation in direct and group Media Studio.
- Direct and multi-person group voice/video calls with screen sharing.

TO STOP
Double-click 2_STOP_WHATSCHAT_V15.cmd.

IMPORTANT
- No Node.js installation is needed; a portable runtime is included.
- Keep the gateway computer on.
- Relay calls are live only and are not saved, but calls are not end-to-end encrypted.
- A free trycloudflare.com address changes whenever the tunnel restarts.
- Do not extract this over an older WhatsChat folder.
- Messages are protected in transit by HTTPS/WSS on the public gateway, but this project is not end-to-end encrypted.
